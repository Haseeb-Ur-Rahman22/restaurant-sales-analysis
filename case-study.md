# Restaurant Sales & Operations Analysis

**The question:** A Hyderabadi restaurant with five outlets had two years of order data and no clear picture of it. What is making money, and where is it leaking?

*Built on a realistic simulated dataset for demonstration.*

## What I found

**The business is growing, and it has a clear season.** Revenue reached about 32.7 crore over two years, with 2025 well ahead of 2024. Sales peak in March and December and dip over the summer.

**Biryani is not the whole story.** Main Course and Starters earn the most, with biryani close behind. The premium dishes carry the revenue. Cheap breakfast and beverage items sell in big numbers but bring in very little.

**The real problem is cancellations, and it lives on the delivery apps.** About 2.4 crore was lost to cancelled orders. Swiggy and Zomato cancel around 9 to 10 percent of orders, while dine in cancels only about 3 percent. Same food, very different outcome by channel.

## What I told the owner to do

1. Fix the delivery app cancellations first. That is where nearly all the lost money is.
2. Promote and protect the high earning dishes, and rethink the items that take up menu space without earning.
3. Run promotions in the slow summer months to even out the year.

## How I worked

I cleaned and checked the data in SQL, built one trusted table to run everything on, then answered each question with SQL and brought it to life in a three page Power BI dashboard: an overview, a menu deep dive, and an operations page.

I keep it honest about limits. This data has no cost, customer, or table information, so it cannot show profit, repeat customers, or table turnover. I say that plainly rather than stretch the data past what it can support.
